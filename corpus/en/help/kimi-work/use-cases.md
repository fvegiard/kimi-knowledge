# Use Cases

## Finance: Investment Strategy Analysis

Have Kimi Work research Warren Buffett's holdings over the past 10 years, summarize his investment strategy, and generate a "Buffett Investing Skill". After installing the Skill, connect it to Kimi's professional financial data sources to analyze whether your own holdings and strategy align with Buffett's value-investing approach.

**Workflow**: Research ▶ Generate Skill ▶ Install Skill ▶ Use the Skill and call professional financial data sources for analysis

## Research: Data Processing and Paper Assistance

Have Kimi Work act as an urban-computing researcher: clean a simulation dataset on your local computer, summarize the key characteristics and patterns, plot academic charts, and then help complete a paper based on the cleaned data and charts.

**Workflow**: Local data cleaning ▶ Chart plotting ▶ Paper assistance

## Office: Market Research and Deliverable Output

Have Kimi Work read the product plans, industrial-design prototypes, and GTM plan in a local folder, then use WebBridge to log into subscribed data-dashboard websites in the browser for research. It conducts in-depth research on the overall market, overseas laws and regulations, and competitors' business status, and — combining a proprietary dashboard — analyzes 2,400+ user reviews to generate a 128-page cross-border e-commerce market analysis report. Then it calls a dedicated PPT Skill to turn the report content and local design prototypes into a presentation deck with one click.

**Workflow**: Read local files ▶ View subscribed databases in the local browser ▶ Generate an in-depth report ▶ Call the PPT Skill ▶ Output the client-facing plan
