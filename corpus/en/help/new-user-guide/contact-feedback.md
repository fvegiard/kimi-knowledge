# Contact and Feedback

## Where can I submit feedback?

Choose the appropriate channel based on the type of feedback:

| Feedback type                            | Channel                                                                                                                                                                   |
|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Product issue feedback                   | On the official website, go to “Account → User Feedback → Help & Feedback” in the lower-left corner; or in the App, go to “Account → Settings → Feedback”                 |
| Chat quality feedback                    | After the chat ends, click “👎” and describe the issue type and what went wrong                                                                                           |
| Account appeals / complaints and reports | [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#ef9c9a9f9f809d9baf828080819c87809bc18c81)                                                                               |
| Media inquiries                          | [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#9fefeddff4f6f2f6b1fcf0f2)                                                                                               |
| Kimi API support                         | Log in to the [Open Platform](https://platform.kimi.com/) to find customer support contact information and the developer community                                        |
| Official WeCom customer support          | Follow the WeChat Official Account “月之暗面Kimi”, then use the bottom menu “Contact Us” - “Contact Customer Support” to find the official customer support WeCom account |
| Investor contact                         | [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#5a363f3d3b362e3f3b371a373535342932352e743b33)                                                                           |
