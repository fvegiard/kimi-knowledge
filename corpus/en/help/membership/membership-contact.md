# Kimi Membership Support Contacts

If you have a membership-related issue, you can get help through the channels below.

## Dedicated Support for Paid Members

**Email**: [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#026f676f606770716a6b72426f6d6d6c716a6d762c616c)

For issues such as:

- Membership benefits not received
- Payment or deduction errors
- Help with plan upgrades or downgrades
- Invoice requests

Emails from paid members will be prioritized. Please include your account information and screenshots of the issue so we can identify the problem more quickly.

## General Support

**Email**: [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c1b2b4b1b1aeb3b581acaeaeafb2a9aeb5efa2af)

For issues such as:

- Questions about product features
- Technical issues during use
- Account-related issues (non-payment)
- Feature suggestions and feedback

## In-Product Feedback

You can also submit feedback directly in Kimi:

![In-product feedback entry point](https://statics.kimi.ai/kimi-helpcenter-doc/en-CN/membership/images/contact-feedback/contact-feedback-01.bf0c99a1334c.png)

- **Web version**: Click the “Feedback” button in the lower-left corner of the page, describe your issue, and submit it.
- **Mobile App**: Go to “Me → Help & Feedback”, then tap “Report an Issue”.

In-product feedback automatically includes your device and account information, which helps us troubleshoot faster.
