# How to Purchase Kimi Business Enterprise

## Purchase online

On your personal homepage, click "Membership Plan" and select "Get Enterprise" to adjust the number of seats directly on the subscription page (the seat selector defaults to 5 and can be set between 2 and 150) and complete your purchase. You can also go directly to the [enterprise purchase page](/membership/pricing?from=help&tab=business) to complete your purchase.

![Kimi membership subscription page - Get Enterprise](https://statics.kimi.ai/kimi-helpcenter-doc/en-CN/kimi-business/images/kimi-business-purchase/subscription-page.c833a838018b.png)

Click 'Get Enterprise' on the Membership Plan page

![Kimi Business - choose the number of seats](https://statics.kimi.ai/kimi-helpcenter-doc/en-CN/kimi-business/images/kimi-business-purchase/enterprise-dialog.88bc3d3d6b84.png)

Choose the number of seats, then click 'Subscribe' to complete the purchase

Kimi Business starts at 2 seats (up to 150 seats per purchase) with an annual subscription, and you can add more seats during the subscription period.

## Contact support

For enterprise-grade data privacy protection, enterprise-exclusive services (including offline enterprise contract signing), dedicated technical support, and other needs, scan the QR code to contact our official support directly.
