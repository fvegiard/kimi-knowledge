# Kimi Business Enterprise Overview

Kimi Business is an intelligent office solution built for enterprise teams. It delivers complete AI capabilities while safeguarding enterprise data and supporting collaborative team management.

Kimi Business is available as an annual subscription, starting from 2 seats with a limit of 150 seats per purchase. It is suitable for small and medium-sized enterprises with team collaboration needs.

## Pricing

| Item             | Details                                                                               |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Price            | ¥4,200/seat/year (about ¥350/seat/month)                                              |
| Minimum purchase | Starts from 2 seats, billed annually from ¥8,400; up to 150 seats per purchase        |
| Billing method   | Annual subscription; additional seats can be purchased during the subscription period |

When you add seats during a subscription period, the price is prorated based on the remaining days from the purchase date to the subscription expiry date. The unified expiry date remains unchanged.

## Core Benefits

- Minimum order of 2 seats
- Your business data is not used for model training by default
- Ultra-long conversation capacity with up to 1M tokens
- All advanced features unlocked: Kimi Work / Swarm / Dashboard / Plugins
- Kimi Code available
- Dedicated enterprise technical support

### Enterprise-Exclusive Features

- **Data privacy protection**: Enterprise data is not used for model training, helping keep business information secure
- **Member management**: Admins can invite and remove members, and flexibly allocate seats
- **Dedicated technical support**: Enterprise customers receive dedicated technical support

## Member Management

### Roles and Permissions

| Role   | Permissions                                                            |
|--------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Admin  | Purchase seats, generate invitation links, manage members, view orders |
| Member | Use enterprise benefits, view personal credit                          |

The account that first purchases the enterprise subscription automatically becomes the admin.

### Invite Members

Admins can generate invitation links and set them to be valid for 2 days or 7 days. After a member clicks the link and accepts the invitation, they can join the organization and use the enterprise benefits.

Each person can join up to 10 enterprise organizations. After joining, a member occupies 1 seat. The same user occupies only 1 seat within an organization. The organization must have unallocated seats before it can invite new members.

### Seat Status

| Status          | Description                                                                                                                                                       |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Total seats     | All unexpired seats purchased by the organization                                                                                                                 |
| Unallocated     | Seats not currently occupied by anyone; these can be used to invite new members                                                                                   |
| Pending release | Seats still occupied in the current cycle after members are removed; these seats cannot be assigned to new members and will be released in the next billing cycle |

### Remove Members

- Removed members immediately lose access to the enterprise workspace
- Once removed, a member immediately loses Kimi Business benefits and can no longer use enterprise credit
- The removed member’s seat enters the "Pending release" status and will be released in the next billing cycle

## Workspaces

Kimi Business introduces workspaces to fully separate personal and enterprise data.

### Workspace Types

- **Personal workspace**: Personal membership benefits, personal chat history, and settings
- **Enterprise workspace**: Enterprise membership benefits and enterprise-related data

After you switch workspaces, the page automatically refreshes and loads the data and benefit configuration for the selected workspace.

Platform support: Kimi Business Enterprise features are available on Web and desktop. Mobile currently supports personal membership only and cannot use Enterprise features.

## Benefit Distribution Rules

### Activation Rules

- **Effective immediately**: After a member accepts an invitation and joins the organization, they immediately receive the current-period benefits
- **Full current-period credit**: When a new member joins using an unallocated seat or newly added seat, they receive the full benefit credit for the current period; it is not prorated by join time or the remaining days of the month
- **Aligned cycles**: The enterprise subscription is settled on a unified subscription cycle, with credit refreshed monthly

### Expiration Rules

- **Member removal**: A removed member immediately loses access to the enterprise workspace and Kimi Business benefits
- **Subscription expiry**: After the organization’s subscription expires, benefits for all members are frozen and will be restored after renewal
