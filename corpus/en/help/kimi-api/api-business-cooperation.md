# Business Cooperation and Sales Contact

Kimi API offers a range of cooperation options for enterprise customers, designed to meet business needs across different company sizes and use cases.

## How do I contact the sales team?

Please visit [platform.kimi.com/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.com/contact-sales) and fill out the contact form. Our sales team will get in touch with you within 1–2 business days.

You can also include the following information in the form so we can respond more quickly:

- Company name and industry
- Estimated usage volume
- Main application scenarios
- Preferred cooperation model

## Algorithm filing

The Kimi LLM has completed the required algorithm filing in mainland China. If you need to view filing information or obtain related materials for compliance purposes, please contact our sales team.

## Contract signing

Enterprise-verified users can sign a formal service contract with Moonshot:

- Standard service agreements and customized contracts are supported.
- After the contract is signed, you can top up via corporate bank transfer and receive enterprise-grade SLA support.
- For the detailed process, please contact our sales team.

## Enterprise discounts

Enterprise customers with high usage volumes can apply for dedicated discounts:

- Discount levels depend on committed usage and the cooperation term.
- Enterprise verification and contract signing are required.
- Please apply through the sales team and provide your estimated monthly usage.

## Enterprise-grade API services

Enterprise-grade API services provide the following on top of the standard API:

- Higher rate limits and concurrency quotas
- Dedicated technical support channel
- Customized SLA coverage
- Priority access to new features

For details, please visit [platform.kimi.com/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.com/contact-sales) to contact our sales team.
