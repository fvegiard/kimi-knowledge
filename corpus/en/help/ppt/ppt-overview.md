# Kimi PPT Overview

## What is Kimi PPT

Kimi PPT is a smart presentation generation tool from Kimi. Powered by the K3 LLM, it automatically optimizes layouts and design to deliver more professional visuals. Simply enter a one-line topic, paste a piece of text, or upload a document, and Kimi PPT will choose the most suitable visual storytelling approach for the content type, then generate a complete presentation with a clear structure and professional design within minutes. Kimi PPT currently supports multi-format input, including PDF, Word, PPTX, Excel, TXT, and images. It has also significantly enhanced image remixing and custom template capabilities, supporting one-click recreation of reference images and creation based on user-uploaded templates, so the output better fits personalized needs.

## Compared with traditional PPT creation

| Dimension          | Traditional PPT                       | Kimi PPT                                                           |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Creation method    | Manual layout and page-by-page design | Generate a complete outline, content, and design from one sentence |
| Time required      | Hours to days                         | Generate a first draft in minutes                                  |
| Content generation | Write the copy yourself               | AI automatically researches, writes, and structures the content    |
| Visual design      | Depends on templates or design skills | AI automatically matches colors, layouts, charts, and icons        |

## Key advantages of Kimi PPT

1.  AI Deep Research + content generation

- Powered by Kimi’s search and long-context understanding capabilities, it automatically completes topic research, data integration, and content writing, producing professional-grade PPTs with one click
- Referenced text and data are checked against real information sources, ensuring the finished PPT is substantive and well-supported

2.  Smart visual design with flexible refinement

- Automatically matches theme colors, fonts, icons, and chart styles, delivering highly usable results from one-click generation
- Optimizes visual presentation at the same time, supports multiple styles and templates, and raises the visual ceiling of your PPT
- After the PPT is generated, you can click to preview it and edit it online, with full manual refinement supported

3.  Ready to use, free to try

- After generation, you can download an editable PPT file or preview and present it online
- A certain amount of free credits is available each month, so you can experience the full functionality without paying
