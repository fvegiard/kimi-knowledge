# Account Suspension and Appeals

## Why was my account suspended?

If you violate laws or regulations, the user agreement, or platform rules, the platform may take the following actions:

- Block the relevant content
- Freeze, close, or transfer the account
- Suspend or revoke some or all entitlements, including paid entitlements
- Refuse, suspend, or stop providing services

If you disagree with the decision, please email [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#fd8e888d8d928f89bd909292938e959289d39e93) to submit an appeal. We will review it and reply as soon as possible.
