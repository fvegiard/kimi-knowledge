# How do I contact Kimi Support?

Choose the channel that matches your issue so we can help you faster.

## Human support (WeCom)

Follow the WeChat Official Account “**月之暗面Kimi**”, then tap “**Contact Us**” → “**Contact Support**” in the bottom menu to find the official WeCom human support team.

When emailing us, please include your account information (registered mobile number or email address) and screenshots of the issue. Emails from paid members will be prioritized, helping us locate the problem more quickly.

## In-product feedback

You can also submit feedback directly in Kimi. The system will automatically include your device and account information to help us investigate:

- **Web**: click “Account → User Feedback → Help & Feedback” in the lower-left corner.
- **Mobile App**: go to “Account → Settings → Feedback”.

## Response quality feedback

If you’re not satisfied with a response, click “👎” below it and briefly describe the issue type and the specific problem. Your feedback helps us keep improving.

For membership-related issues, you can also see [Membership issue contact information](/en/help/membership/membership-contact). For media enquiries, please email [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#29595b6942404440074a4644).
